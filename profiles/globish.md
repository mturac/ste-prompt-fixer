# globish profile — 1500-word ceiling

For readers who are not native English speakers.

1. Use only common international English words. Treat 1500 words as a hard ceiling.
2. No idioms, no phrasal-verb chains, no culturally local references.
3. Max 15 words per sentence.
4. Numbers as digits. Dates as ISO (2026-10-23). Units explicit ("10 km", not "10").
5. Active voice, present tense preferred.
6. Technical terms allowed and kept unchanged; gloss them once on first use.

Use for: multilingual teams, export documentation, support articles for global users.
